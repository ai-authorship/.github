# AI Authorship

AI is changing how people write, review, and translate text. Readers often have little context about those contributions, and creators have no simple, shared way to describe them.

AI Authorship exists to make that process easier to communicate. Our [AI Authorship Labels](https://ai-authorship.github.io/) provide short, linked descriptions of how humans and AI contributed to a piece of text—from human writing with AI proofreading to AI drafting with human review.

The labels are voluntary and self-reported. They do not verify how content was produced or guarantee its accuracy; their value depends on honest disclosure.

The system is open to adaptation. Explore the labels, use them in your own work, or adapt the project to reflect your own writing process under its [CC BY 4.0 license](https://ai-authorship.github.io/LICENSE.html).
